Two papers accepted to <a href="https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2026">CVPR</a>: (1) <a href="https://arthurmoreau.github.io/OffTheGrid/">OffTheGrid</a>, a new approach to allocate primitives freely outside pixel/voxel grids, and (2) <a href="https://charge-benchmark.github.io/">Charge</a>, a comprehensive dynamic dataset and benchmark for 3D & 4D.
